Professional Certifications

Staying relevant in cybersecurity requires continuous learning and industry-recognized credentials:

  • CompTIA Security+: Baseline certification covering fundamental security principles and risk management.
  • Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP): Advanced credential for security leaders and architects.
  • Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH): Focuses on penetration testing methodologies and proactive threat vulnerability scanning.

Recommended Learning Resources

  • Cybrary & TryHackMe: Hands-on virtual labs for real-world cyber threat simulation.
  • SANS Institute: Industry-standard training courses on incident response and digital forensics.
