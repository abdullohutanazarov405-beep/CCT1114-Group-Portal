The CIA Triad

The core foundation of information security relies on three core tenets known as the CIA Triad:

  • Confidentiality: Ensuring sensitive data is accessible only to authorized personnel through encryption and access control.
  • Integrity: Safeguarding the accuracy and completeness of information by preventing unauthorized modification or tampering.
  • Availability: Guaranteeing that systems, networks, and data are dependable and accessible when needed by authorized users.

Common Workplace Cyber Threats

Modern organizations face an evolving landscape of digital threats targeting both technical infrastructure and human factors:

  • Phishing & Social Engineering: Deceptive emails and communications designed to trick employees into revealing credentials or sensitive data.
  • Ransomware: Malicious software that encrypts organizational files and demands payment for the decryption key.
  • Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attacks: Eavesdropping on unencrypted communications over insecure public or workplace Wi-Fi networks.
  • Insider Threats: Unauthorized data exposure caused by careless employee habits or malicious internal actors.
